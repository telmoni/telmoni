A Slack or Discord connector posts project [notifications](/docs/integrations/notifications)
directly into a specified channel.

Connectors are configured per project. Project **Owners** and **Admins** can
connect and manage destinations from the project's **Connectors** page
(`/{organization}/{project}/connectors`). Project **Members** can view the
project's connectors and their delivery histories.

## Connecting a channel

1. Open your project's **Connectors** page.
2. Select **Connect Slack** or **Connect Discord**.
3. You will be directed to Slack or Discord's authorization screen. Choose the
   destination workspace and target channel.
4. Once authorized, you are redirected back to the **Connectors** page, where
   the channel shows as **Connected**. Connecting raises a `connector_connected`
   notice, which the channel receives as its first message.

Authorization handshakes must be completed within 10 minutes.

**Send test** on a connected channel posts one message, titled "Slack is
connected" or "Discord is connected", that reads "This is a test from Telmoni.
Notices for this project will arrive here." The test is not recorded in the
project's [audit log](/docs/workspace/audit-log) or its delivery log.

Slack shows each notice as a header with its title, its body, and its kind
below them; Discord shows it as an embed with the title, the body, and the kind
as its footer.

On a self-hosted deployment, a vendor whose app credentials the operator has
not configured shows **Soon** on its tile, with **Connect** turned off (see
[Slack and Discord apps](/docs/self-host/configuration#slack-and-discord-apps)).

## Connection states

The Connectors page shows each channel in one of three states:

- **Connected**: notices are posted as they arrive.
- **Needs attention** (Slack only): the installation stands, but the channel
  refuses posts: it was archived or deleted, or Slack refuses posting into the
  workspace's general channel. Notices queued for it fail.
- **Disconnected**: the installation itself is gone: the Telmoni app was
  removed from the Slack workspace or its token revoked, or the Discord webhook
  was deleted or its token invalidated (Discord answers `401`, `403` or
  `404`). Notices queued for it fail.

In either failed state, **Reconnect** on the row runs the vendor's
authorization again. Picking the same channel reconnects it in place, and the
audit log notes the reconnection; picking another channel makes a new
connection. No notice is queued for a connection while it is in a failed
state, so reconnecting does not deliver the notices raised meanwhile.

## Permissions and security

Telmoni requests one scope from each vendor, the one that lets it post into the
channel you pick:
- **Slack**: `incoming-webhook`
- **Discord**: `webhook.incoming`

It requests no scope for reading messages, listing channels, or listing
workspace members. The grants and webhook targets it receives are stored
encrypted at rest.

## Delivery and retries

A notice's first attempt starts as soon as the notice is written, unless 32
first attempts are already in flight on that server; then the delivery loop
takes it on its next poll. If a delivery attempt fails due to
a transient network error, a provider rate limit, a server error on the
provider's side, or, on Slack, a `408` timeout:

- Telmoni makes up to **5 attempts** in all: after 30 seconds, then 2, 8, and 32
  minutes. These are the defaults, which the hosted service uses and a
  self-hosted operator can change (`NOTIFICATIONS_DELIVERY_*` in the
  [configuration reference](/docs/self-host/configuration)).
- If the provider answers with a `Retry-After` header (or, on Discord, a
  `retry_after` in the body), Telmoni waits at least that long before the next
  attempt, up to a day.

A refusal that says the channel or the installation is gone moves the
connection to **Needs attention** or **Disconnected**, as the
[states](#connection-states) above describe. On Slack, so does a disabled
workspace, a revoked token, or an app no longer allowed to post there. Any
other refusal fails that notice at once, without retries, and leaves the
connection **Connected**.

## Disconnection

If the vendor answers that the destination is gone (the channel was deleted or
archived, the incoming webhook was removed, or the Telmoni application was
uninstalled from the workspace), Telmoni marks the connector **Needs
attention** or **Disconnected** as the [states](#connection-states) above
describe, fails the notices still queued for it, and posts a
`connector_disconnected` notice to the project's feed and its other connectors.

Removing the Telmoni application from a Slack workspace disconnects all channels
connected to that Slack workspace. The event is recorded in the organization's
[audit log](/docs/workspace/audit-log) with `external:slack` as the actor.

## Disconnecting manually

To remove a connection, select **Disconnect** on the connector's row on the
**Connectors** page. Telmoni deletes the connection with its queue and delivery
log, records the action in the project's audit log, and tells the vendor: a
Discord webhook is deleted, and the Slack app is uninstalled from the workspace
once no other connection on the platform uses it.
