---
title: "Notifications"
description: "What Telmoni records in activity feeds, where notices are displayed, and how notifications are dispatched to external destinations."
---

A notification is an event record describing an action or status update within
an organization or one of its projects: a member joining, or a connector state
change. Telmoni writes each notice to its feed, and queues one delivery for each
connection that takes it, in one database transaction, and makes no send before
that transaction commits.

## Notice types

| Kind | Raised when |
|---|---|
| `member_added` | Someone accepts an invitation to the project |
| `member_left` | A member leaves a project or is removed from it, including when they leave or are removed from its organization |
| `connector_connected` | A Slack, Discord, or webhook connector finishes setup |
| `connector_disconnected` | A connection stops: the vendor refuses posts to it for good (see [connection states](/docs/integrations/slack-and-discord#connection-states)), a webhook answers `410` or fails ten sends in a row, or Slack reports the Telmoni app removed from a workspace or its tokens revoked |
| `organization_alert` | A notice for the organization's Owner and Admins, posted to the organization rather than a project; the open core raises none |

## Where you read them

- **Project feed**: The **Recent activity** section of each project's
  **Overview** page surfaces project notices, the latest 50. Every project role
  can read the feed.
- **Invitations and offers**: Invitations to you, and offers of an organization
  or a project, appear separately in the console header bell and on your
  account's **Notifications** page (`/account/notifications`).

Selecting **Mark read** marks every unread notice in that feed as read, for
everyone who reads it.

## Forwarding to external connectors

Projects can forward notifications to third-party endpoints through connectors
configured on the project's **Connectors** page
(`/{organization}/{project}/connectors`):

- **[Slack and Discord](/docs/integrations/slack-and-discord)**: Direct channel
  notifications where the people in your project already read.
- **[Webhooks](/docs/integrations/webhooks)**: HTTPS POST requests with HMAC-SHA256
  signatures delivered directly to your own infrastructure.

Connectors deliver notices asynchronously. Each delivery is a queued row of its
own: a send that fails for a reason a retry can fix (a network error, a timeout,
a rate limit, a server error) goes back in the queue behind a backoff, up to five
attempts by default, and the notice on the feed is a separate row that no
delivery outcome changes. Where the `connectors` feature flag is off for the
deployment, starting a Slack or Discord handshake, or connecting a webhook, is
refused (a handshake already under way still completes), and retries wait until
it is on again. A notice's first attempt is still made as it is raised while
fewer than 32 first attempts are in flight on that server (past that, it waits
with the retries), and **Send test** and **Resend** still go out. The flag is
read for the whole deployment: an organization's own setting for it has no
effect.

## Retention

- **Feed notices**: Retained for **90 days**.
- **Connector delivery logs**: Kept for **30 days** after each delivery was
  last delivered or failed.
