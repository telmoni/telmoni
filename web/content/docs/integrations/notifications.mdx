---
title: "Notifications"
description: "What Telmoni records in activity feeds, where notices are displayed, and how notifications are dispatched to external destinations."
---

A notification is an event record describing an action or status update within
an organization or one of its projects: a member joining, a connector state
change, a monitor a ping made, a ping key the rotation retired, or an
incident opened or resolved on a monitor. Telmoni writes each notice to its
feed, and queues one delivery for each connection
that takes it, in one database transaction, and makes no send before that
transaction commits.

## Notice types

| Kind | Raised when |
|---|---|
| `member_added` | Someone joins the project: they accept an invitation, or an organization Owner or Admin adds them from the organization |
| `member_left` | A member leaves a project or is removed from it, including when they leave or are removed from its organization |
| `connector_connected` | A Slack or webhook connector finishes setup |
| `connector_disconnected` | A connection stops: Slack refuses posts to it for good (see [connection states](/docs/integrations/slack#connection-states)), a webhook answers `410` or fails ten sends in a row, or Slack reports the Telmoni app removed from a workspace or its tokens revoked |
| `monitor_discovered` | A ping under one of the project's ping keys names a monitor the project does not have, or a run an SDK sends names an agent no monitor has: the monitor is made, paused as new, and watches for nothing until you set it up |
| `ping_key_retired` | The rotation retires one of the project's ping keys: a newer key stands, and this one has not pinged in over a week, or was minted over a week ago and never used, so a line still using it is refused from then on |
| `incident_opened` | One of the project's monitors needs someone: it failed, stalled, crossed a bound or is late. One incident stands open per monitor however many runs fail under it, so the notice comes once per spell of trouble; a monitor that flips in and out of trouble more than six times in an hour is flapping, and its notice says so |
| `incident_resolved` | The monitor is healthy again, or its owner paused it, and the incident closed; the notice says how long it stood. A flapping monitor's incident closes once it has held one state for an hour |
| `organization_alert` | A notice for the organization's Owner and Admins, posted to the organization rather than a project. The hosted service raises one a day for each ceiling of the plan that refused the organization while it lasts: its monitors' pings past the minute's ceiling, its data out past the day's, or a monitor a ping or a run would have made past the plan's monitors, with the count and when it began; a self-hosted deployment raises none |

## Where you read them

- **Project notices**: notices posted to a project appear on its **Overview**
  (`/{organization}/{project}`) for anyone on the project, newest first, each
  unread one in bold. **Mark read** there marks every unread one as read, for
  everyone on the project. They also reach every channel the project connects
  that takes their kind (Slack, a webhook subscribed to it). Where the
  deployment runs the console agent, it searches them, and the project's
  delivery log, when asked.
- **Organization notices**: notices posted to the organization rather than a
  project appear on its **Overview** for its Owner and Admins. **Mark read**
  there marks every unread one as read, for everyone who reads them.
- **Invitations, offers and projects you were added to**: Invitations to you,
  offers of an organization or a project, and projects an organization's Owner
  or an Admin added you to, appear separately in the console header bell and on
  your account's **Notifications** page (`/account/notifications`).

## Forwarding to external connectors

Projects can forward notifications to third-party endpoints through connectors
configured on the project's **Connectors** page
(`/{organization}/{project}/connectors`):

- **[Slack](/docs/integrations/slack)**: Direct channel notifications where
  the people in your project already read.
- **[Webhooks](/docs/integrations/webhooks)**: HTTPS POST requests with HMAC-SHA256
  signatures delivered directly to your own infrastructure.

Connectors deliver notices asynchronously. Each delivery is a queued row of its
own: a send that fails for a reason a retry can fix (a network error, a timeout,
a rate limit, a server error) goes back in the queue behind a backoff, up to five
attempts by default, and the notice on the feed is a separate row that no
delivery outcome changes. Where the `connectors` feature flag is off for the
deployment, starting a Slack handshake, or connecting a webhook, is
refused (a handshake already under way still completes), and retries wait until
it is on again. A notice's first attempt is still made as it is raised while
fewer than 32 first attempts are in flight on that server (past that, it waits
with the retries), and **Send test** and **Resend** still go out. The flag is
read for the whole deployment: an organization's own setting for it has no
effect.

## Retention

- **Feed notices**: Retained for **90 days**.
- **Connector delivery logs**: Kept for **30 days** after each delivery was
  last delivered or failed.
