A **trace** is one run's record: the run's own span and every span recorded
inside it, found by the run's id. A project's **Traces** page lists them; each
opens beside the list, and has a page of its own. Everything on both is
metadata — timings, statuses, counts, model and tool names — and nothing on
either is a prompt, a completion or a tool payload, in any
[content mode](/docs/api/ingest#what-is-dropped-at-the-door).

## The list

Every run of the project's agents from the last seven days, newest first, one
row each, fifty to a page:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| Trace | The first eight characters of the run's id; opens the trace beside the list, as a click anywhere else on the row does |
| Started | When the run's first span started, in your time zone |
| Agent | The run's agent (`telmoni.agent`) |
| User | The end user the run was for (`user.id`), as stored: hashed under the project's own key |
| Session | The session the run belongs to (`gen_ai.conversation.id`), as the application set it |
| Status | **OK**, **Error** or **Cancelled** once the run's own span has arrived; **Running** until it has |
| Steps | How many steps the run took; hover for its model and tool calls |
| Tokens | Its model calls' input and output tokens, summed; hover for the split |
| Duration | From its first span's start to its last span's end |

**Columns** shows or hides each of these, and more beside them: when the
run's last span ended, the deployment its spans were sent from
(**Environment**: `production`, `staging`) and its release (**Release**: a
version or a commit), each once where a run crossed services deployed apart,
the class of the error it ended on (**Error**: `TimeoutError`,
`RateLimitError` — never its message), its model calls, tool calls, errors
and spans, its input, output and cached tokens, and the models and tools its
calls used.
The counts and the tokens are each a group that opens under its heading,
which says how many of it show; a column whose name does not say what it
holds says so behind its ⓘ; and the trace's id always shows. Your columns,
and how tightly the rows sit (**Row density**), are kept in your browser
rather than in the page's link.

### Filters

The **Filters** panel beside the list names each agent, status, model, tool,
user, session, environment, release, tag and prompt among the window's runs,
with how many runs carry it — counted under every other filter, so a value
beside a ticked one says what ticking it would add. Tick values to narrow the
list: the values of one filter add to each other, and filters narrow
together. The panel names fifty values of each at most, the most runs first;
a session's id can be typed in as well (**Text**), so can a user's as it is
stored, the hashed id the User column shows, and so can a tag, as
`key:value`. A run carries an environment, a release, a tag or a prompt
where any of its spans does: the deployment is the resource's
`deployment.environment.name` and `service.version`, a tag each
`telmoni.tag.<key>` with its value, and a prompt a span's
`gen_ai.prompt.name`. Click an agent, a user, a session, a model, a tool, an
environment or a release in a row to narrow the list to it alone. The filters
in force stand above the rows: clear one with its ×, or all of them at once.
The button beside the panel's heading hides it, and opens it again.

**Errors**, **Slow** and **Tokens** beside it are ready-made: the runs that
ended in error, the longest runs first, or the runs that used the most tokens
first. Click one again to take it off.

### Sort, pages and the window

**Sort** by clicking a column's heading; every column sorts but Trace, Ended,
Spans, Cache read, Cache write, Models and Tools. A second click turns it
round. Names sort in the alphabet's order, and the newest, the longest and the
most come first on the others.

**Rows per page** is 25, 50 or 100; beside it, the total the filters admit,
and the way to the first, previous, next and last page. A list past a
thousand pages is reached by narrowing the window or the filters.

**The window**, beside the page's title, is the last day, week, 30 or 90
days, by the day each span arrived. Above the rows, its runs stand as bars by when they started — how
they ended, or their tokens, or the median and 95th percentile of their
durations, as the menu above the bars picks — each bar an hour, a few hours,
a day or a week, starting on your own clock. **Chart** shows the three as
charts in the table's place.

As the project's runs land, a bar above the list counts the new traces rather
than moving the rows under your hand; **Show** reads the list again, with them
on it where the filters admit them. An empty list shows its first trace at
once. A trace's own page draws itself again as its run's spans land, while it
is in view: at once for the first, and once more for whatever arrives in the
next 30 seconds.

**Refresh**, beside the window, reads the list now; beside it, have the page
read itself again every 30 seconds, minute, 5 or 15 minutes as well, for a
console whose live events are paused.

Every filter, the sort, the window and the page are in the page's URL, so a
link holds the view you were looking at.

### Saved views

**Views** keeps a list as the project's own: its filters, sort, window, rows
per page and columns, under a name. **Save as a new view**, then pick it from
the menu to open the list as it was saved, with its columns. A view the list
has moved off since — a filter or a column changed — is starred, and **Save
changes** writes it over. Every member of the project applies its views; its
Owners and Admins save, rename and delete them
([Roles](/docs/workspace/roles#project-roles)). A project keeps up to 100,
and up to 100 [layouts](/docs/workspace/sessions#keeping-a-layout) of a
session's page and a trace's beside them.

### Downloading the list

**Download** writes the list as a file, CSV for a spreadsheet or JSON: every
run its filters admit, sorted as it is, up to the first 10,000. Tick rows by
their boxes to download those alone. A file holds what the list holds —
metadata, with each user's id as stored — and never a message.

## A trace

A click on a row of the list, anywhere but on one of its links, opens the
run's trace in a panel over the list's right side, under the page's header and
beside the sidebar, without leaving the list. The row stays marked while its
trace is open. The panel's ↑ and ↓ open the previous and the next row's
trace, as `k` and `j` do. Drag the panel's left edge to make it wider or
narrower, and double-click the edge to put it back; **Full size** fills the
page's area with the panel and **Exit full size** puts it back. Your browser
keeps both for the next trace you open. The arrow beside them opens the
trace's own page. **Close**,
`Esc` from inside the panel, or your browser's **Back** shuts it, and the
page's link holds the open trace, so a link you share opens the list with it.
A `⌘`-click or `Ctrl`-click on the run's id opens the trace's page in a new
tab instead. The trace's own page shows the same, with the page's room.

Its first line sums the run: its status, when it started, how long it ran, its
model calls' tokens — hover for the split — its session, a link to
[its page](/docs/workspace/sessions#a-session), its user, a link to the runs
that share it, and its tags.

Below it, the run's spans stand as the tree they ran in, each under the span
it names as its parent: its kind, its name, how long it ran, a model call's
tokens, and, for a span that failed, the error's type in red. **Search spans**
finds spans by name, kind, model, provider or error type, each with the spans
above it.
**Timeline** draws each span as a bar on one line of time, from the run's
first start to its last end. The arrow keys walk the tree: up and down a span,
right to open a span's branch or step into it, left to shut it or step out of
it. A run of more than 400 spans opens with its branches shut but the run's
own.

Beside the tree stands the span you choose, the run's own first: its kind, its
status and the error's type, its model and provider, the environment and
release it was sent from, the tool it called — the model, the environment,
the release and the tool each a link to the list narrowed to them — its
tokens, input, output and cached, when it started and ended to the
millisecond, how long it ran, when it was received, and its ids. The run's own span adds the
run's steps, calls and tokens, its user, session and tags, the bounds it
declared and its sample rate. **Attributes** lists what else was kept with the
span under its own name, and **Raw** shows either as it was stored. The
trace holds a run's first 5,000 spans, fewer where their names, tags and
attributes pass about 8 MiB; a run with more says so under them.

A run whose own span has not arrived yet is shown as running, and its trace
is read again as more of its spans land. A trace that is not in the project —
a run that has passed its retention, or one of another project's — says so.

### Layouts

The trace's own page opens in the same layout as
[a session's](/docs/workspace/sessions#customize), changed by **Customize**
beside its title: to begin with the tree holds the run's own row alone, its
details beside it, until Customize opens its steps or calls, the branches
with a failure inside, and the timeline. The depth and the
calls shown narrow what the tree lists, what starts open opens its branches,
and the pinned spans stand above the tree, each a press away from being
chosen, wherever the depth leaves them. **Layouts** keeps a cut under a name
for everyone on the project and sets what the project opens in, as the
session's page does. The trace opened over the list keeps the tree whole.
