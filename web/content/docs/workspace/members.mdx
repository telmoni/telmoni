title: "Members and invitations"
description: "Inviting people to an organization or project, adding a member of the organization to a project, accepting and declining, changing roles, removing members, and leaving."
---

Collaborators join an organization or project by invitation, or, on a project,
by a seat given to somebody already in its organization. Either grants a
[role](/docs/workspace/roles) of **Admin** or **Member**.

## Scope of an invitation

Telmoni supports two invitation scopes:

1. **Organization invitations**: Invited from the organization's **Members** page
   (`/{organization}/members`). Grants an organization role (`Admin` or
   `Member`). An invited Organization Admin gains administrative access across
   all projects in the organization.
2. **Project invitations**: Invited from a project's **Members** page
   (`/{organization}/{project}/members`). Grants access directly to that
   project as an `Admin` or `Member` without granting organization-wide
   permissions.

## Inviting someone

An Owner or Admin can send invitations:

1. Navigate to the **Members** page of the organization or project.
2. Select **Invite member** (on a project's page, **Add member**, and **By
   email** where the organization's members are offered beside it), enter the
   recipient's email address, and choose a role (**Admin** or **Member**).
3. Telmoni emails an invitation link (on a self-hosted deployment, when the
   operator has configured mail), and the console displays the link once so
   you can share it directly.

**An invitation is created for any email address that is not already on the
roster, whether or not it currently holds a Telmoni account.** Inviting checks
the address against the roster (for a project, its seats) and against your own
address, never against existing accounts, and the answer is the same either
way. An address already
on that roster is refused, your own included. A project invitation checks the
project's seats, so the organization Owner's address, which holds none, is
refused only when the invitation is accepted. A new invitation to an address
that already holds a live one from the same project or organization replaces
it, and the earlier link stops working.

Where the `members` feature flag is off, for the deployment or for the
organization, inviting is refused with "Adding a member is switched off right
now."

Invitations expire automatically after **7 days**.

On the hosted service, an organization on the Hobby plan holds one member,
its owner: an invitation from it, or from one of its projects, is refused,
naming Pro. Its **Members** page says so in place of the invite button, and a
project's under **By email**, closed, while **From the organization** stays
open to whoever in the organization holds no seat there yet
([Billing](https://telmoni.com/docs/workspace/billing#one-member-on-hobby)).
A self-hosted installation holds no organization to a number.

## Adding a member from the organization

An organization Owner or Admin can seat somebody already in the organization
on one of its projects without an invitation. On the project's **Members**
page, **Add member** offers **From the organization**: the organization's
Members who hold no seat on that project yet (its Owner and Admins are on every
project already), with a role (**Admin** or **Member**). The person is added at
once — there is nothing to accept, since they agreed to the organization when
they joined it — and told by email (on a self-hosted deployment, when the
operator has configured mail) and in their notifications: the bell and the
**Notifications** page list the project until they open it, from there or any
other way, or dismiss it, or the project moves to another organization, or
they are made an Admin of the organization, which puts them on every project.
An invitation of theirs to that project is withdrawn by the seat. The seat is
recorded in the audit log, and the project's **Overview** notes who joined.

A project Admin who is only a Member of the organization sees no roster and
invites by email. Where the `members` feature flag is off, or the organization
is paused on the hosted service, adding is refused as inviting is, and nobody
whose account is being deleted is added.

## Accepting or declining

Invitations can be answered in two places:

- **The console bell & notifications**: Pending invitations appear in the bell icon
  in the console header and on your account's **Notifications** page
  (`/account/notifications`). You can accept or decline directly without
  leaving your current view — when your email address is verified. An address
  nobody has verified (a password sign-up on a deployment with `VERIFY_EMAIL`
  off, or an identity provider that did not vouch for it) sees no invitations
  here and uses the email link, which is itself the proof that the inbox is
  yours.
- **The email link**: Opens a page naming who invited you, the organization (for
  a project invitation, that it is for a project in that organization), and the
  role granted, with **Accept invitation**. You must sign in or register with
  the exact email address the invitation was sent to.

A signed-in recipient with a verified address also sees the invitation arrive
as a toast, "Invitation to join" and the organization's or project's name,
with **Review**, which opens the Notifications page; the console's live stream
carries no invitation to an unverified address. The bell accepts and declines
in one press; the Notifications page asks you to confirm a decline.

Accepting an invitation records an audit event. Accepting a project invitation
also posts a `member_added` notice on the project's **Overview** and to its
connectors, and lists you on the organization's roster as a **Member**: no
organization powers come with that, and it is what lets the organization show
your address on its rosters. Declining an invitation ends it immediately.

## Withdrawing a pending invitation

An Owner or Admin can withdraw a pending invitation at any time from the
**Members** page by selecting **Withdraw** on its row. Withdrawn links are
invalidated immediately.

## Changing roles and removing members

An Owner or Admin can change a member's role or remove them from the
**Members** page:

- **Role changes**: Changing between Admin and Member takes effect immediately
  and is recorded in the audit log.
- **Removing members**: Removing a member revokes their access immediately. A
  console page they have open in that project or organization leaves it at
  once, for the console's entry page.
- **The Owner cannot be removed or demoted**: The Owner's row is marked **Owner**
  and carries no removal controls. To change the Owner, the current Owner must
  initiate an [ownership transfer](/docs/workspace/organizations-and-projects#transferring-organization-ownership).

## Leaving an organization or project

- Anyone but the Owner can leave an organization from its **Overview** page
  (`/{organization}`), which also removes them from every project in it.
- A project seat is given up with **Leave** on your own row of the project's
  **Members** page, whether the seat was accepted from an invitation or given
  from the organization's roster, and whatever its role; you stay on the
  organization's roster, and an Owner or Admin can add you back. An
  organization Admin who still holds a seat on a project, from before they
  were made an Admin, also finds **Leave** beside it on the organization's
  **Projects** page (`/{organization}/projects`), which opens for the Owner
  and Admins alone; they keep their access to the project through their
  organization role. The Owner, who holds every project outright, is offered
  no Leave anywhere.
- The Owner cannot leave until ownership is transferred to an Admin or the
  organization is deleted.

## Invitation limits

A project can send up to **20 invitations in any 24 hours**,
withdrawn ones included, and its organization's Owner and Admins can add up to
**20** of the organization's members to it in any 24 hours, removed ones
included. Organization invitations have no such count. There is
no limit on the number of members in an organization or project, apart from
Hobby's one on the hosted service
([One member on Hobby](https://telmoni.com/docs/workspace/billing#one-member-on-hobby)).
On the hosted service, a paused organization sends no invitation until it
moves to a paid plan
([One Hobby organization](https://telmoni.com/docs/workspace/billing#one-hobby-organization)).
