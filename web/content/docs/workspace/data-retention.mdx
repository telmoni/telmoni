---
title: "Data retention"
description: "How long Telmoni keeps each kind of data — telemetry, the organization's records, accounts and sessions, the console agent's data — what ends each window, and what the hosted service keeps in backups and logs."
---

Telmoni keeps each kind of data for a window of its own. The windows are the
platform's: the same on a self-hosted deployment and on the hosted service,
and none is set per project yet. Deleting a project, an organization or an
account ends every window early for what they held, as
[Data deletion](/docs/workspace/data-deletion) describes.

## Telemetry

Telemetry is kept only while its project is active.

| Data | Kept | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Spans: every run, step, model call and tool call, as metadata | 360 days after a span arrives | `telmoni-server rotate` drops each day of spans once every span in it is older |
| Hourly totals: spans, units, tokens, the cost calls reported and durations by project, agent, kind, model, provider, tool, status, environment and release, which name no user and no tag | 13 months | `rotate` drops each month of totals once it is older |
| A deleted project's spans and totals | Until the project is deleted | Marked deleted the moment the project goes, and gone from every read within moments, as the store applies the mark; `rotate` rewrites them off the disk that night |
| The claim on an export's bytes, which answers a retry with the first answer rather than writing twice | 1 hour | Expires |
| A project's telemetry settings: its content mode, and the key its end-user ids are hashed under | While the project exists | Purged with the project |
| A project's saved views of its traces, sessions and users: each one's name, filters, sort, window and columns | Until an Owner or Admin deletes it, or the project goes | Purged with the project |
| A project's saved layouts of its sessions and traces: each one's name, options and columns, and the layout the project opens in | Until an Owner or Admin deletes it, or the project goes | Purged with the project |
| A project's [alerts](/docs/workspace/alerts): each one's definition, the connectors it tells and its last value | Until a member deletes it, or the project goes | Deleted with its incidents and its maintenance windows, or purged with the project |
| An alert's incidents: their severity, who acknowledged or resolved them, where their notices went and the runs they showed | While their alert exists | Deleted with the alert |
| Maintenance windows, kept after they end as the record of who muted what | While their alert, or their project, exists | Deleted with it |

Content — prompts, completions, system instructions, tool inputs and outputs,
retrieved documents, error messages and every attribute that names a person —
is kept for no time at all: it is dropped at the door in the one content mode
offered today, as the [ingest page](/docs/api/ingest#what-is-dropped-at-the-door)
lists. An end user's id is stored only as a keyed hash
([Users](/docs/workspace/users)).

## The organization's records

| Data | Kept | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Organizations, projects, members and roles | While they exist | Deleted with the organization, or the project |
| Invitations | Until accepted, declined or withdrawn, or 7 days; an unaccepted one's record 30 days past expiry or withdrawal, an accepted one's until its acceptor's account is erased or its project or organization is deleted | The retention sweep deletes an unaccepted one's record |
| API keys | While live; a revoked or expired key's record 7 days | The retention sweep deletes the record |
| Connectors: Slack grants, webhook endpoints and their signing secrets | While connected; a Slack bot token until Slack has taken its app back, a day at most | Erased when disconnected, or with the project or the organization, the Slack app uninstalled from a workspace no other connection uses |
| Activity feed notices | 90 days | The notifications sweep deletes them |
| Connector deliveries: what was sent where, and the answer | 30 days | The same sweep |
| The audit log | Without a time limit, through the organization's deletion | Never deleted: its monthly partitions are kept, and only an export's file is a copy |
| Audit log exports | 7 days after the file is built | The audit-export sweep deletes the file |

## Accounts and sessions

| Data | Kept | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Your account: the name and address your identity provider or password account gave | While the account exists | Erased when you delete the account |
| Sessions | Until signed out or revoked; one unused for 30 days can no longer be resumed, but its row stays until you end it or delete your account; a revoked session's row 90 days | The retention sweep deletes a revoked session's row |
| Sign-in codes, device authorizations and the tokens nobody can use any more | Usable for minutes; the daily retention sweep deletes them once expired or spent, a spent refresh token after a day | The retention sweep deletes them |
| Confirmation codes, for a deletion or an email change | 15 minutes | Expire; five wrong guesses burn one |

## The console agent

| Data | Kept | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Your conversations with the agent, visible to you alone | 90 days after a conversation was last used | The agent's retention sweep deletes them |
| Its search index: copies of the audit log, feed notices, connector deliveries and your own answered questions | 730, 90, 30 and 90 days: each copy no longer than its source | The same sweep |
| The documentation a deployment indexes | Until it is updated or removed from the corpus | Re-indexed |

What the agent sends its model provider is on the account's
[Privacy page](/docs/account/privacy#console-agent-data).

## On the hosted service

- **Backups.** The database is backed up daily, with seven days of
  point-in-time recovery, and thirty daily backups are kept. Telemetry's
  store is backed up nightly, and none of its backups is kept past thirty
  days. Deleted data leaves the backups as they expire, within thirty days.
- **Operational logs** are kept thirty days. They name ids, never addresses.
- **The console agent's model provider** keeps each request 55 days to detect
  abuse, as the [Subprocessors](https://telmoni.com/docs/legal/subprocessors) page states.

The hosted service's commitments are in its
[Privacy Policy](https://telmoni.com/docs/legal/privacy-policy).

## On a self-hosted deployment

The windows above hold as long as `telmoni-server rotate` runs daily and the server's
sweeps run, as the [production guide](/docs/self-host/production#partition-rotation)
describes: spans and totals go when `rotate` drops them, and the rest when
the sweeps inside `telmoni-server serve` pass. Your backups are yours, and so is
their window.
