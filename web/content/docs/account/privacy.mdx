---
title: "Privacy and deletion"
description: "Managing active sessions, product analytics, and permanent account deletion."
---

Your account's **Privacy** page (`/account/privacy`) manages your sessions,
your account's deletion and, where the deployment offers it, your analytics
preference.

## Active sessions

The **Active sessions** panel lists every session of your account that has not
been signed out or revoked, one that lapsed after 30 days unused included: a
device label derived from the browser or CLI (such as `Chrome (macOS)` or
`Telmoni CLI (Linux)`), when each session started, and when it was last seen.

You can end any session but the current one from here; the device signs out
within a few minutes. To end your current session, sign out from the user
menu.

## Product analytics

A **Data collection** switch appears on this page only on a deployment whose
operator has configured an analytics provider (`ANALYTICS_INGEST_URL` and
`ANALYTICS_WRITE_KEY` in the [configuration
reference](/docs/self-host/configuration#web-console-nextjs-bff)). It is **off by
default** and records your choice. The console sends nothing to that provider,
whatever the switch says: its one analytics event, written with your user ID at
every browser sign-in, goes to its own operational log and is not sent on.
Where no provider is configured, the page has no switch.

## Console agent data

A person's conversations with the console agent (their questions and its answers)
are visible only to them, and are kept for 90 days after the conversation was
last used.

The agent's search index holds copies of the audit log, activity feed notices,
connector deliveries, each person's own answered questions and, where the
deployment indexes it, the documentation. A self-hosted deployment indexes
this site's documentation unless `DOCS_CORPUS_URL` is `off`; the hosted
service indexes none. Each copy is kept no longer than its source's own
retention window:
- **Audit log**: 730 days
- **Activity feed notices**: 90 days
- **Connector deliveries**: 30 days
- **Conversations**: 90 days, visible to their author only
- **Documentation**: Retained until updated or removed from the published corpus

Each question is sent to the model provider configured for the deployment,
together with the earlier turns of the conversation (up to 20 messages) and
whatever the agent's tools read to answer it: the passages it retrieved, and the
member roster, connectors, delivery logs, or audit events it looked up. Search
queries and the indexed text go to the configured embeddings endpoint, and to
the rerank endpoint if one is set. On the hosted service, the model and the
embeddings are Google's Gemini API, on Google's paid terms, as the
[Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page states.

## Deleting an organization

Only an organization **Owner** can delete an organization. Deletion is initiated
from the **Danger zone** in the organization's **Settings** page
(`/{organization}/settings`):

1. Request a 6-digit confirmation code, which is emailed to your address and
   remains valid for 15 minutes.
2. Enter the confirmation code to confirm deletion.
3. Access ends immediately for all members, and all API keys stop functioning.

The automated deletion sweep then permanently erases the organization, its
projects, its agent conversations and search index, and everything else it
held, usually within the hour. There is no restore window and no undo. Its
audit chain is kept.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is permanent and performed from the **Danger zone** on your
account's **Privacy** page (`/account/privacy`).

### Ownership check

**Account deletion is refused while you own an organization that has other
members.** You must first either:
- [Transfer ownership](/docs/workspace/organizations-and-projects#transferring-organization-ownership)
  to an Admin.
- Remove all other members from the organization.
- Or delete the organization from its settings page.

### Deletion procedure

1. Select **Send confirmation code** to receive a 6-digit code by email (valid
   for 15 minutes). Five wrong guesses burn it; **Resend code** issues another.
2. Enter the code and select **Permanently delete my account**. The ownership
   check above is made at this step. Your access ends at once, and the erasure
   follows, usually within the hour.

### What happens when your account is deleted

- **Organizations you exclusively own**: Marked for deletion at once, with their
  API keys revoked, and purged by the deletion sweep after the same 15-minute
  grace as any organization's deletion.
- **Other organizations you belong to**: You are removed from all other
  organizations and projects. Their audit logs record your departure.
- **Audit log exports**: Every export you started is deleted, files and all.
- **Console agent**: Deletes your conversations with the agent and your own
  index rows, deletes the index passages that quoted your address or your name,
  and rewrites you to "a former member" in other people's conversations. A
  one-word display name is not searched for, since it would match other people.
- **Notifications**: Feed notices referencing your account are anonymized
  (e.g., "A former member joined the project").
- **Authentication**: Your user record is purged and all your sessions end
  immediately. On a self-hosted deployment, a password account's hash goes
  with it, while an OpenID Connect provider keeps its own record of you; on
  the hosted service, your account at its identity provider is deleted too.
