title: "Accessibility"
description: "Turning letter-key shortcuts off, showing keyboard focus, reducing motion, and increasing contrast."
---

Your account's **Accessibility** page (`/account/accessibility`) holds the
settings that change how the console answers the keyboard and how it looks.
Each applies the moment you change it, and each is saved in the browser you
change it in, as the theme and corners are: another browser or device keeps
its own. They apply to the console, these docs and the site alike.

## Keyboard

- **Letter-key shortcuts**: on by default. Single keys such as `g` followed by
  a letter, `c` for a page's main action, `/` for search and `?` for the
  shortcuts sheet act on their own. Turn them off if you use voice control
  or a screen reader that passes keys to the page, so a dictated word never
  opens a page or starts an action. Shortcuts held with `⌘` (`Ctrl` on Windows
  and Linux) keep working, and the shortcuts sheet then lists only those.
- **Show focus**: off by default. When on, whatever has keyboard focus carries
  a ring in the text colour, so you can see where you are as you move with
  `Tab` and the arrow keys. A field you type in shows it however you reached
  it; clicking a button or a link never draws it.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: opens the sheet of every shortcut that works on the
  page you are on.

## Display

**Reduce motion** and **Increase contrast** each offer **System**, **On** and
**Off**. **System**, the default, follows your device's own setting, and the
page says what your device asks for right now.

- **Reduce motion**: menus, the sidebar and dialogs appear at once instead of
  sliding and fading.
- **Increase contrast**: secondary text is darker in the light theme and
  lighter in the dark one, past 9:1 against the page and 6.5:1 or more on
  every surface it sits on, and the edges of fields, cards and dividers stand
  out at 3:1 or more against the page and the cards they sit on.

Something in the console hard to reach or to read? Tell us what you browse
with, at the support address the page gives where the deployment has one.
