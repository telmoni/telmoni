A project's **Spend** page (`/{organization}/{project}/dashboards`) charts
its runs over the window the picker beside the title chooses — the last day,
week, 30, 90 or 360 days, by the hour for a day and by the UTC day for longer —
from the totals Telmoni keeps an hour at a time, thirteen months on every plan
whatever it keeps of the spans:

| Chart | What it draws |
|---|---|
| Spend | What the model calls cost, each as it reported, else its tokens at the project's own price or the price table's ([What a call cost](/docs/api/ingest#what-a-call-cost)), scaled by its run's [sample rate](/docs/api/ingest#what-is-dropped-at-the-door) where a plan sampled it |
| Tokens | The model calls' input and output tokens |
| Runs | The runs, each in the hour its own span arrived, those that ended well and those that failed |
| Latency | The runs' durations at the median, the 95th and the 99th percentile |
| Time to first token | How soon the runs' first model calls began to answer, at the median and the 95th percentile, read from the spans, which alone keep it, so within the project's retention |

Above them, each of the project's running [budgets](/docs/monitoring/alerts#a-budget):
what the month had spent of it when the budget last read it, or **Not read
yet** for one that has not.

Spend counts every model call the project's spans hold.

## Spend split over time

**Charts** beside the window adds or takes off each chart, and adds spend
split by one of:

| Split | Read from |
|---|---|
| Model, agent, provider, environment or release | The hourly totals, as far back as the window reaches |
| Tag, by one key the window's traces carry | The spans, no further back than your plan keeps them |
| User or session | The spans, no further back than your plan keeps them |

A split chart stacks each hour's or day's spend by value: the seven values
that spent most over the window each in a colour of their own, and the rest
as **Other**. Under it, every value's spend, tokens, runs and failures, the
costliest first; a user's row opens the Runs list narrowed to their runs, and
a session's its runs in the order they started. A call counts for its run's
value: a tag as the run's own span carries it, else as any of
its spans does, and a run begun the day before the window still counts for
its user, session or tag. Spans of a run that carries none count under
**None**. A split from the spans names the ten values that spent most and
sums the rest as **Other values**, so a project of many users stays readable;
where your plan keeps the spans for less than the window, the chart says how
far back it reads.

## Saved dashboards

The charts drawn are in the page's URL, so a link opens the same charts.
**Dashboards** beside them saves the charts as a dashboard under a name, up
to twelve charts each, for everyone on the project to open; owners and admins
save, rename and delete them, and every role opens them (see
[Roles](/docs/workspace/roles)). A dashboard keeps its charts and nothing
else: the window is the picker's, and no user's or session's value is kept
in it. With none named, the page opens on the cost dashboard: spend, tokens,
runs, latency and time to first token.

The same totals, by the hour or the day and split, are
[`GET /v1/metrics`](/docs/api/reference#get-v1metrics).
