---
title: "Dashboards"
description: "A project's Dashboards page: its spend, tokens, runs and their failures, latency and time to first token over a window, from the hourly totals, its budgets this month above, and each model's, agent's, provider's, environment's or release's totals below."
---

A project's **Dashboards** page (`/{organization}/{project}/dashboards`) charts
its runs over the window the picker beside the title chooses — the last day,
week, 30 or 90 days, by the hour for a day and by the UTC day for longer —
from the totals Telmoni keeps an hour at a time:

| Chart | What it draws |
|---|---|
| Spend | What the model calls cost, each as it reported, else its tokens at the project's own price or the price table's ([What a call cost](/docs/api/ingest#what-a-call-cost)) |
| Tokens | The model calls' input and output tokens |
| Runs | The runs, each in the hour its own span arrived, those that ended well and those that failed |
| Latency | The runs' durations at the median, the 95th and the 99th percentile |
| Time to first token | How soon the runs' first model calls began to answer, at the median and the 95th percentile, read from the spans, which alone keep it, so within the project's retention |

Above them, each of the project's running [budgets](/docs/monitoring/alerts#a-budget):
what the month had spent of it when the budget last read it, or **Not read
yet** for one that has not. **Group** beside the window splits the
window's totals by model, agent, provider, environment or release, the
costliest first, each with its tokens, runs and failures.

Everyone on the project reads the page. The same totals, by the hour or the
day and grouped, are [`GET /v1/metrics`](/docs/api/reference#get-v1metrics).
