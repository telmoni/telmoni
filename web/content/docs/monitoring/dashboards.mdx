title: "Spend"
description: "A project's Spend page: its spend, tokens, traces and their failures, latency and time to first token over a window, with its budgets this month above."
---

A project's **Spend** page (`/{organization}/{project}/dashboards`) charts
its traces over the window the picker beside the title chooses — the last day,
week, 30, 90 or 360 days, by the hour for a day and by the UTC day for longer —
from the totals Telmoni keeps an hour at a time, thirteen months on every plan
whatever it keeps of the spans:

| Chart | What it draws |
|---|---|
| Spend | What the model calls cost, each as it reported, else its tokens at the project's own price or the price table's ([What a call cost](/docs/api/ingest#what-a-call-cost)), scaled by its trace's [sample rate](/docs/api/ingest#what-is-dropped-at-the-door) where a plan sampled it |
| Tokens | Five exclusive segments: input, cache read, cache write, output and reasoning, for all models or the model the picker selects |
| Traces | The traces, each in the hour its agent's own span arrived, those that ended well and those that failed |
| Latency | The traces' durations at the median, the 95th and the 99th percentile |
| Time to first token | How soon the traces' first model calls began to answer, at the median and the 95th percentile, read from the spans, which alone keep it, so within the project's retention |

Above them, each of the project's running [budgets](/docs/monitoring/alerts#a-budget):
what the month had spent of it when the budget last read it, or **Not read
yet** for one that has not.

Spend counts every model call the project's spans hold.

The Tokens chart subtracts cache reads and writes from the input segment and
reasoning from the output segment; the five segments sum to inclusive input
plus output for consistent provider counts. Subtraction stops at zero if the
reported subsets exceed their totals. **Cache share** is the window's cache-read
tokens divided by its input tokens, never an average of call percentages;
it is **—** where no input was reported. Cache writes are not cache hits.
The model picker narrows both the segments and this share, and is drawn
where the window holds more than one model. Where the window's models cannot
be read whole, the chart draws every model's tokens together, without it.

The same totals, by the hour or the day and split, are
[`GET /v1/metrics`](/docs/api/reference#get-v1metrics).
