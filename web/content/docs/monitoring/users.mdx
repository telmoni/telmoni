A **user** is whoever a trace was for: your application's own customer or
colleague, never a member of your organization, who are on **Members**. An
application names one when it opens a trace — `user_id` in the
[SDKs](/docs/api/sdks), `user.id` on the span — and every trace that carries
the same id is that user's
([Sessions and users](/docs/monitoring/traces#sessions-and-users)). A trace
that names none belongs to none.

The id is stored hashed under the project's own key, so no page here names
anybody: a user is shown by the first eight characters of that hash, and found
by the id your application sends (**Your id**, under [Filters](#filters)). A
project admin erases one, by the same id, on the project's **Settings** page
([Data deletion](/docs/workspace/data-deletion#deleting-a-trace-or-an-end-user)).

A project's **Users** page (`/users`) lists them. Where
[Traces](/docs/monitoring/traces) answers for one execution and
[Sessions](/docs/monitoring/sessions) for one conversation, this page answers
for a person: how often your agents worked for them, how that went and what
it cost. Anyone on the project reads it, and nothing on it is a message: the
figures are summed from the traces' metadata.

## The list

Every user with a trace in the last seven days, the one last heard from first,
one row each, fifty to a page:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| User | The first eight characters of the user's id as stored. It opens [the user's own page](#a-users-page), as a click anywhere else on the row does |
| Agent | The agents whose traces were for the user |
| Last trace | When their last trace in the window ended, and how the trace that started last stands: **OK**, **Error** or **Cancelled**, or **Running** until that trace's own span has arrived |
| Sessions | How many [sessions](/docs/monitoring/sessions) their traces named in the window, each once. It opens **Sessions**, narrowed to the user |
| Traces | How many traces were for them in the window, those still running among them |
| Failed | How many of those ended in error |
| Tokens | Their traces' input and output tokens, summed; hover for the split |
| Spend | What their traces' model calls cost, each priced as **Traces** prices it |

**Columns** shows or hides each of these, and three more: how long their
traces ran, summed (**Duration**), when their first trace in the window
started (**First trace**), and their traces'
[tags](/docs/monitoring/traces#tags) (**Tags**). Your columns are kept in your
browser, as the trace list's are.

A user is summed over the window: their traces from before it are no part of
their row, so a longer window shows more of a user who has been with you long.

## Filters

The **Filters** panel is the trace list's, with the same filters, each value
counted in users: how many hold a trace that carries it. A user is listed
where **any one** of their traces passes every filter in force, and their
figures stay those of **all** their traces in the window. So **Status: Error**
lists the users for whom a trace ended in error, each with their whole count
and cost, never the failed traces' alone. The
[score](/docs/monitoring/scores) filter works the same way, by a score given
to one of the user's traces or their spans, or to one of their sessions.

A user or a session is found by the id your application sends (**Your id**),
as on [Traces](/docs/monitoring/traces#filters). Click an agent or a tag in a
row to narrow the list to it.

**Errors**, **Slow** and **Tokens** beside the window are ready-made: the
users holding a trace that ended in error, those whose traces ran longest
first, or those that used the most tokens first.

## Sort, pages and the window

**Sort** by clicking a heading: **Last trace**, **Sessions**, **Traces**,
**Failed**, **Duration**, **Tokens**, **Spend** or **First trace**. A second
click turns it round. The latest and the most come first, but **First
trace**, which opens on the earliest.

**Rows per page** is 25, 50 or 100; beside it, the total the filters admit.
**The window**, beside the page's title, is the last day, week, 30 or 90 days.
Every filter, the sort, the window and the page are in the page's URL, so a
link holds the view you were looking at.

The list draws itself again as the project's traces land, while it is in view,
as **Sessions** does. **Refresh** reads it now.

## Downloading the list

**Download** writes the list as a file, CSV for a spreadsheet or JSON: every
user its filters admit, sorted as it is, up to the first 10,000. A row holds
the user's id as stored and what the list sums of them, their sessions,
agents, traces, tokens, spend, times and tags, and no content.

## A user's page

A user's id opens their own page wherever it stands: in a row here, on
**Traces** or on **Sessions**, over that list's window; and on a trace's
first line, over a window that reaches back to the trace.

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Last trace | When their last trace ended and how it stands; **Open it** opens it beside the page |
| All traces | Their traces over the window, and how many ended in error, with their share |
| Sessions | How many sessions their traces named, with the way to those sessions on **Sessions** |
| Spend | What their traces cost over the window, priced as **Traces** prices them, and their tokens in and out |
| Agents and tags | The agents that worked for them, each opening [the agent's own page](/docs/workspace/organizations-and-projects#an-agents-page), and their traces' tags, each opening **Users** narrowed to it |
| Over the window | Their traces as bars by when they started, on your own clock: how they ended, their faults, tokens, cost, durations, times to first token and output speed, two charts to a row |
| Sessions | Their five sessions last heard from, each summed over all of its traces and opening [its own page](/docs/monitoring/sessions#a-sessions-page), with the way to the rest |
| Traces | Their 25 newest traces, each opening beside the page as a row of **Traces** does, with the way to the rest on **Traces** |

A part that could not be read says so, and the rest stand. A user with no
trace in the window says so, and leaves the window to widen. The page draws
itself again as the project's traces land.
