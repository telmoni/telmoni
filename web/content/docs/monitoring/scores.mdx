title: "Scores"
description: "Scores on a run, a span or a session: from your code, through an SDK, OpenTelemetry's evaluation event or the API, or from a member on a trace's page; each a number, a yes or no, or categories, shown on the trace, in the trace list's Scores column and at the head of a session."
---

A **score** judges a run, one of its spans or a session: a name, 1 to 64
characters, and a value of one of three types.

| Type | Value |
|---|---|
| Number | Any finite number, on a range where the score names one |
| Yes or no | 1 or 0 |
| Category | One to ten categories, each up to 64 characters |

A score is metadata, kept in every
[content mode](/docs/workspace/organizations-and-projects#a-projects-content-mode)
for as long as the spans it judges, and it leaves with them: a trace deleted,
an end user erased, a project deleted, or its days past retention.

## Where scores come from

- **Your code**, on any project: our SDKs' `score`, on a handle while its
  span is open and on the client once it has ended
  ([Scores](/docs/api/sdks#scores) on the SDK page). Underneath, a score of a
  span still open is OpenTelemetry's `gen_ai.evaluation.result` event on the
  span it judges, the run's where the event is on the run's own span
  ([Scores](/docs/api/ingest#scores) on the ingest page), which any exporter
  sends, another exporter's spans' scores landing with their run; and one of
  something already ended, a session's among them, goes to
  [`POST /v1/scores`](/docs/api/reference#post-v1scores), under its own id so
  a retry keeps it once.
- **A member**, on a trace's page: **Score** under the trace's line takes a
  name, a type and a value. A member's score of one name replaces their last
  of that name on the trace, and their **×** takes it back; a project's owners
  and admins take back any member's. Each is filed in the organization's
  [audit log](/docs/workspace/audit-log) as a change to the trace, by its
  name, never its value.

## A score's comment

A member may say why, in a **Comment** of up to 4,000 characters. A comment
is content, unlike its score: it is kept only on a project
whose [content mode](/docs/workspace/organizations-and-projects#a-projects-content-mode)
is on — a member's is refused on one that keeps none — sealed under the
project's key with the run's own content, and opened only with it, so a
trace's page shows it, from the score's badge, where it shows the run's
content, and the read is filed as that content's is; a trace's download
carries it with the run's content.
Scoring again replaces it, a score given again without one takes it back, and
it goes with its score and its run, and past the project's retention from
when it was given. A session's score takes none: no run of the session holds
it; nor does one given once the organization holds the content its plan does.

## Where they show

- **A trace's page**: every score of the run and of its spans under the
  trace's line, a span's named by its span, each saying who gave it, its
  comment opened from it; and a span's beside the span when it is chosen.
- **The trace list**: the **Scores** column, on in the default columns, gives
  each run's own scores by name, a number's mean where there are several, a
  yes or no's share of yeses, and a category's values. **Score** in the
  filters, or `?score=` and a name, narrows the list to runs carrying that
  score, an optional least and most (`score_min`, `score_max`) bounding its
  value; a run passes where any score of the name falls within them. Narrowed
  to a score, the list's charts add its mean over the window, each bar the
  scores given in it on the runs the list admits.
- **An alert**: a [score alert](/docs/monitoring/alerts#making-an-alert)
  watches a score's average, a percentile, its highest or how many over a
  window, or its change from the window before, so a drop opens an incident.
