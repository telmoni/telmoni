title: "General"
description: "The console's theme, its corners, and how tightly the lists' rows sit."
---

Your **General** settings page (`/settings/general`) sets how the console
looks. Each setting applies the moment you change it, and each is saved in the
browser you change it in: another browser or device keeps its own.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **System** follows your device's light or dark setting; **Light**
  and **Dark** hold one or the other whatever the device says.
- **Corners**: **Rounded** or **Sharp** edges on cards, fields and buttons.

## Lists

- **Row density**: **Comfortable** or **Compact** rows on the
  [Traces](/docs/monitoring/traces) list; compact fits more on the screen.

The [Accessibility](/docs/settings/accessibility) page holds the settings that
change how the console answers the keyboard, its motion and its contrast.
