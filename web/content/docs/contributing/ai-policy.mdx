---
title: "AI policy"
description: "Use whatever tools help you, AI among them, and understand every line you submit."
---

Use whatever tools help you, AI among them. The one rule: **understand every line you submit.** Whatever drafted a change, it is yours once you open the pull request: know what it does and why, and be ready to explain it and answer a reviewer's questions yourself.

AI-assisted work is reviewed like any other: a change its author can't explain is sent back, however good it looks. We're glad to help you learn what you need to get it through.
